Terms of Use & 10 Platform Rules

Separate rules for using QR Studio under SUREWAL.TECH for positive office, educational, and project purposes.

This platform is built for constructive and lawful use such as office workflows, academic/educational materials, student or research projects, portfolios, event flyers, and legitimate work or study links.

Rule 2: Strict Ban on Nudity & Explicit Content

Nudity, sexually explicit, pornographic, or adult content is strictly prohibited across all QR links, photos, videos, and documents.

Rule 3: Privacy & Explicit Consent

Do not upload or share photos, videos, documents, or personal information of another person without their permission, and avoid uploading sensitive or confidential personal records.

Rule 4: No Harassment, Hate, or Abuse

Harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, violence, or targeted abuse is strictly forbidden.

Rule 5: No Malware, Phishing, or Deceptive Links

Do not upload infected/malicious files or create QR codes pointing to phishing pages, scams, deceptive redirects, or harmful scripts.

Administrators reserve the right to remove QR records, metadata, or hosted files that violate these rules or raise safety, privacy, or legal concerns.
