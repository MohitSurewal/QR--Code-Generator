Privacy Policy

How QR Studio Tech handles your links, uploaded files, QR codes, and metadata.

1. No User Account Required

Public visitors can generate QR codes for links, photos, videos, and supported documents without creating a user account. We do not expose any public list or search feed of other users' links or uploads.

2. Storage & Long-Lasting QR Codes

QR code PNG images are generated in memory rather than stored permanently on local server disk. Uploaded photos, videos, and documents are hosted on Cloudinary so your QR links remain accessible, while upload metadata (such as file type, folder path, size, and timestamp) is recorded in Firestore for operational and admin oversight.

3. Security & Access Control

All form submissions are protected with CSRF tokens, session cookies are restricted, and admin features are protected behind authenticated admin credentials.
